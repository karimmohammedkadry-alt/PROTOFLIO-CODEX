CODEX PORTFOLIO — GOOGLE FORM DELIVERY SETUP

The contact form is present in the website, but it will not deliver messages until you connect a Google Apps Script Web App endpoint.

1) Open https://script.google.com and create a new project.
2) Copy the contents of google-apps-script.gs into the editor.
3) Replace PUT_YOUR_EMAIL_HERE with the Google account/email where you want inquiry notifications.
4) Save, then Deploy > New deployment > select Web app.
5) Execute as: Me. Access: Anyone (the public form needs access). Review the Google deployment security prompt.
6) Deploy, authorize, and copy the Web App URL ending in /exec.
7) Open script.js and replace PASTE_GOOGLE_APPS_SCRIPT_WEB_APP_URL_HERE with that URL.
8) Publish the updated portfolio files. Form submissions will be recorded in a Google Sheet named CODEX Portfolio Inquiries, and emailed to the configured address.
